3. **current cognitive relation evidence** must be re-earned from fresh post-BOOT lived interaction.

`DURABLE_HISTORY != EXECUTABLE_BODY_REAUTHORIZATION`.
`BODY_REATTACHMENT != COGNITIVE_REDERIVATION`.
