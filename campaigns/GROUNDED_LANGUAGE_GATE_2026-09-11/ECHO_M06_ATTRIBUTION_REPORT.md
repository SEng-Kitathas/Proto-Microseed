# Echo-M06 Attribution — Independent Neutral Subrole Composition

## Why this discriminator is sharper
M05 could still be explained by learning a monolithic whole-role template from full counterpart models. M06 explicitly removes that explanation.

The named-counterpart subrole is learned only from counterparts with a current call-name preference and no recurrence. The recurrent-counterpart subrole is learned only from separate counterparts with recurrence and no call-name preference. The two training cohorts are disjoint, and no training counterpart may instantiate the full target conjunction.

## Held-out recombination
A novel held-out counterpart with both earned facets is recognized through composition of the independently learned subrole schemas. Heldout name-only and recurrence-only counterparts refuse. Social words such as `friend`, `trustworthy`, or `important` cannot substitute for the missing grounded subrole. Seen training counterparts and wrong epochs refuse. Forged schema evidence refuses.

## Scientific force
The held-out success cannot be explained by memorizing a whole named+recurrent counterpart template because no joint exemplar existed in training. This is evidence for systematic relational recombination of independently earned neutral social structure.

## Ceiling
`SYSTEMATIC_NEUTRAL_SUBROLE_RECOMBINATION != GENERAL_SOCIAL_REASONING_OR_RELATIONSHIP_TRUTH`. No friendship, trust, attachment, importance, personality, social valence, relationship type, or human identity truth is earned.
