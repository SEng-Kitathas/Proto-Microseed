# Grounded Language Gate — Tentative Research Campaign — 2026-09-11

## Operator authority

> Language may enable communication, coordination, compression, and human-readable expression, but language SHALL NOT become the hidden source of competence that the grounded/prelingual substrate has not earned. If evidence suggests capability is being smuggled through the language surface, FREEZE the language branch and audit before continuing.

## First campaign objective
Test whether already-grounded referent competence can acquire a readable language surface **without changing the underlying noncommunicative competence**. The initial experiment is readout/communication only: a readable token may name/report an already-grounded referent relation, but may not create the relation, choose truth, grant execution authority, or substitute for missing grounded evidence.

## Freeze rule
Any P0/P1 anti-smuggling violation freezes this branch before further language capability work.

Historical MS702–MS801 language/predicate/event-frame work remains donor-only and must be re-earned under current grounding laws.

## Operator clarification — capability versus smuggling
Language is expected to enable real cognition in some regimes. Concept grasping, causal connection, abstraction, composition, memory organization, and planning may legitimately improve through language. This does not violate the gate. The campaign freezes only when language is acting as an ungrounded/unowned shortcut, oracle, hidden state, or authority bypass rather than an earned mechanism.
