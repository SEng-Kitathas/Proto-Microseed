# Echo-M07 Attribution — Role-Conditioned Held-Out Response Prediction

## Why this discriminator is sharper
M06 demonstrated systematic role recombination but could still be criticized as structural classification. M07 forces the earned role to have predictive utility.

The same opaque stimulus is presented across role-bearing calibration counterparts and non-role controls. Role-bearing calibration contexts consistently produce one opaque response while controls consistently produce a different response. This makes a global stimulus→response shortcut impossible.

## Held-out test
A novel counterpart first earns the composed M06 role. Its response to the target stimulus must be completely unobserved at prediction time. M07 predicts the role-conditioned response, then fresh actual response evidence is recorded afterward. Matching reality passes. A counterexample produces an explicit holdout violation; the system has no authority to rewrite the role or narratively repair the prediction.

## Hostiles
Predictor derivation refuses if role responses are inconsistent, controls do not differ, cohorts overlap semantically with the role, or evidence is missing. Prediction refuses if the heldout response was already observed, if the counterpart does not satisfy the role, if the epoch is stale, or if predictor evidence is forged. Social words cannot substitute for grounded role membership.

## Ceiling
`ROLE_CONDITIONED_HELDOUT_RESPONSE_PREDICTION != GENERAL_SOCIAL_REASONING_PLANNING_OR_RELATIONSHIP_TRUTH`. No friendship, trust, attachment, importance, personality, social valence, relationship type, human identity truth, planner, or general social-reasoning authority is earned.
