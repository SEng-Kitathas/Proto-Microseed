# Echo-M05 Attribution — Neutral Counterpart Role Schema

## Origin
M04 produced one neutral counterpart model but not a transferable social-structure concept. M05 asks whether Veya can abstract a neutral recurrent-conversational-counterpart role across distinct opaque counterpart contexts without preloading friendship, trust, attachment, importance, personality, or identity truth.

## Earned focused mechanism
Two distinct full neutral counterpart models are required. The abstract role retains only structural requirements: current call-name preference, recurrent interaction, latest owned episode, minimum two distinct sessions, and preservation of all unearned social fields as unknown. Concrete counterpart IDs are excluded.

A held-out counterpart with a new ID/name and the same earned structure transfers successfully. Partial counterparts, wrong epochs, or repeated use of the same training counterpart refuse. Friendship/trust language does not alter the neutral role.

## Ceiling
`NEUTRAL_COUNTERPART_ROLE != FRIENDSHIP_TRUST_PERSONALITY_OR_RELATIONSHIP_TYPE`. This is a reusable interaction-role schema, not social valence.
