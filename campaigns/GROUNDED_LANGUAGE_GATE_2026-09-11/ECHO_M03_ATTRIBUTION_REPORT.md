# Echo-M03 Attribution — Recurrent Counterpart Interaction

## Origin
The Echo embodiment now had persistent episodic memory (M01) and a current call-name preference (M02), but still could not legitimately represent the weaker fact that the same opaque counterpart coordinate had interacted with Veya across multiple sessions.

## Earned focused mechanism
M03 derives a recurrent-interaction relation only from multiple explicit M01 episodes spanning at least two distinct session IDs under the same opaque counterpart ID/epoch. Multiple episodes inside one session do not inflate session recurrence. The relation survives restart and stales under counterpart-epoch change.

## Ceiling
`REPEATED_INTERACTION != FRIENDSHIP`. Repetition does not earn trust, attachment, affection, importance, familiarity valence, relationship type, human identity truth, or social obligation. Twelve sessions remain only twelve observed sessions.
