# P03 Attribution Report — Grounded Language-Mediated Concept Formation

## Existing grounded competence
P03 reuses already-earned operational referent grounding and C06 affordance-relative directional relation episodes. Those mechanisms provide current, grounded relational experience; language does not manufacture them.

## New language-mediated cognitive mechanism
Language now participates in concept acquisition: observed surface tokens group multiple grounded relation-sequence exemplars. Veya derives a second-order relation-transform invariant from each group and stores the resulting concept set as exact owned evidence. The lexical token becomes an index into that owned concept state.

This is a real cognitive gain, and it is allowed. It is not classified as smuggling merely because removing language removes lexical retrieval.

## Grounded concept content
Concept content is independent of spelling. Its identity is derived from the grounded relation-sequence transform, not the word. Swapping surface tokens swaps lexical bindings while preserving the underlying concept-content digests.

## Held-out causal use
A resolved learned concept predicts the second grounded directional relation from the first on held-out sequences. This is a causal/use test, not just label reconstruction.

## State ownership and provenance
The concept set is appended to Veya's own evidence store with exact training/holdout sequence hashes and a grounding-dependency fingerprint. Forged/unowned evidence is refused. Edited exemplar payloads whose content hashes do not match are refused.

## Currentness
Grounding dependency drift makes old lexical concept use require revalidation. Fresh grounding may legitimately recover; a concept whose grounded transform agrees with fresh evidence can be revalidated and used again. Drift does not erase learned history, nor is stale premise reuse silently allowed.

## Language-disabled ablation
Removing the language index removes lexical retrieval but leaves the owned grounded concept contents inspectable. `LANGUAGE_DISABLED_PERFORMANCE_DROP != CAPABILITY_SMUGGLING` is therefore realized concretely here.

## External-oracle hostile
Text-only claims, personality slogans, operator assertions, or word spelling cannot create a concept without language-grouped grounded acquisition history.

- ADE-H03: repeated identity/personality wording cannot install a concept — PASS.
- ADE-H08: surface remapping changes lexical indexing without changing grounded concept content — PASS.
