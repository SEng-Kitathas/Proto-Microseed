# Methodology — C-SCALE-LEARN-03A

## Question
Can a higher request/effect policy develop from actual nested outcome experience through an already-developed middle controller, rather than requiring a separately prequalified flat top controller?

## Falsifier
The discriminator fails scientifically if any of the following is necessary:
- preloading top action/outcome relations or routing from a flat controller;
- exposing leaf target/local means to the top;
- adding a hierarchy manager, recursive planner, or atomic hierarchy whole;
- bypassing actual outcome recording;
- changing production Microseed code merely to make the research harness pass.

## Developmental control
Before nested training the top must have no action/outcome predictive relations and no projection-conditioned routing bindings. Opaque target-token projection and bound request interface may be externally qualified and are recorded as assistance.

## Nested evidence path
The middle selects a current capability once per opaque request class. Repeated middle samples then use unique execution episodes, preserving deterministic rehearsal-registry invariants while recording actual subordinate outcomes. Top effects are recorded only at the top boundary.

## Context-projection split scar
The flat and nested top produce semantically identical owned raw-projection sample multisets but different opaque sample IDs/order. Train/validation ordering is therefore made invariant to sample ID. The ordering key uses raw tokens, action token, scope, frame, epoch, and source-projection epochs; it explicitly excludes `effect_token`. The same seeded shuffle and the original MS2063 discovery thresholds are retained.

## Nonclaim
The experiment does not remove external qualification, the predeveloped-middle assumption, or the evaluator-owned top-request-to-middle-context mapping.
