# Project History and Engineering Decisions

Date: 2026-08-30 ET
Scope: compact but load-bearing project history. For exact detail, consult the embedded MS1944 handoff, current maintenance-note snapshot, sealed methodology files in the Git repository, and `THIS_CONVERSATION.md`.

## 1. Canonical versus research lineage
The most important historical distinction is structural, not chronological.

- **Canonical Main-Dev** remains MS1527.
- **Research baseline** carried forward from MS1887.
- **GitHub `main`** historically remained at MS1939 during much of the research-descendant work.
- **Research branch `research/ms1888-replay`** became the publication surface for later sealed research.
- Local research descendants repeatedly ran ahead of public research before being selectively published.

This separation was deliberate so exploratory/research results could be attacked without silently becoming canonical architecture.

Persistent law:
`PROJECT_IDENTITY != CANONICAL_MAIN_DEV != RESEARCH_DESCENDANT != EXPERIMENT`.

## 2. Research OS / methodology correction
The project adopted a process where research and implementation are not isolated phases. The Research ↔ Reality dual-arm doctrine restored the intended metabolism:

`PROBE -> DERIVE -> EMBODY -> VERIFY -> RECURSE`.

The engineering process learned to distinguish:
- discussion from implementation;
- candidate mechanism from earned mechanism;
- test/tool execution from task success;
- local seal from publication;
- publication from canonical promotion.

The R3.1 RAHL engineering archive was later introduced as controlled input and adopted only through project-local shadow/use adjudication. Its compression was never allowed to inherit authority simply because it was newer or shorter.

## 3. MS1940–MS1942 — prelingual signaling without semantic promotion

## 17. MS1996 — caller-free endogenous program construction
MS1996 was a major compositional result.

Important results:
- `K-17 -> M-23 -> R-41` generated endogenously;

- decision-bearing/information-bearing distinctions preserved;
- proposal inert until ordinary action authorization;
- stale primitive removes the old program;

- equal discriminating alternatives produce `MULTIPLE_CURRENT_EPISTEMIC_OPPORTUNITIES`.

Decision:
Do not introduce a planner/semantic action registry when the existing composition machinery can generate the program.

`ENDOGENOUS_CANDIDATE_CONSTRUCTION != EXECUTION_AUTHORITY`.

## 21. MS2000 — same-ID capability requalification
MS2000 lawfully closed the MS1999 stale-reentry seam.

- exact capability ID;
- immutable contract signature;
- exact stale epoch;

Requalification evidence may restore currentness of previously earned authority; it must not grant new authority.

`REQUALIFICATION_EVIDENCE != NEW_AUTHORITY_GRANT`.

## 22. MS2001/MS2002 — multi-restart lifetime + persisted operational referent classes
The multi-restart lifetime layer composed drift/relearning, restart/currentness, projection routing, zero-row reentry, and co-present capability requalification over multiple sessions.

MS2002 then reused the append-only evidence ledger rather than adding a referent registry, so operational referent signature classes could persist through restart/crossing/occlusion.

Important boundaries:
- multiple identical witnesses remain a class/set; no witness is chosen as “the object”;
- incomplete ledger search returns search-budget exhaustion, not false absence;
- complete no-match returns UNKNOWN;
- fresh executable environment authority is still required after restart;
- evaluator mode does not become organism evidence.

Decision:
Persist operational referent evidence in existing evidence/history ownership before inventing a referent registry.

Still not earned:
semantic reference, numerical identity, general autonomy.

## 25. Long-term decisions that should survive future milestones
1. Composition first; prove insufficiency before adding a manager.
2. Preserve UNKNOWN/refusal with named reasons.
3. Keep actual outcome evidence above predicted/intended outcomes.
4. Currentness is separate from historical validity.
5. Qualification is separate from derivation.
6. Execution authority is separate from epistemic need.
7. Operational persistence/reference-like behavior is separate from semantic/numerical identity.
8. Historical negatives remain true at their seal even when successors close them.
9. Research publication is not canonical promotion.
10. Language stays behind the prelingual/reference gate.
11. The reality embodiment arm runs now, not “after research is done.”
12. A fresh successor should continue from the strongest live verified node, not the easiest old checkpoint to understand.
