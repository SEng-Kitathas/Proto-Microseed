## Scientific truth labels
Use the following internally and in handoffs:

### VERIFIED
Directly supported by current artifacts/tests/logs/Git/readback with appropriate scope.

### OBSERVED
Seen in an experiment/tool output but not yet fully qualified/sealed for the stronger claim.

### INFERRED
Best explanation from evidence, not directly established.

### HYPOTHESIZED
Proposed mechanism/claim awaiting pressure.

### UNKNOWN
Evidence does not entitle a conclusion.

Do not promote by tone.
