# Commander’s Intent — ProtoAGI Microseed

Date captured: 2026-08-30 ET
Authority class: operator intent / project-direction continuity
This document does not override stronger scientific evidence or sealed lineage. It explains what the project is trying to accomplish and what properties must survive implementation choices.

## Mission
Build an **honest prelingual developmental cognitive substrate** that can increasingly originate useful cognition from its own grounded history and lawful composition, while preserving explicit limits on truth, currentness, qualification, scope, and execution authority.

The goal is not to bolt a chatbot, planner, semantic ontology, or orchestrator onto a research toy and call the result intelligence.

The goal is to discover how far a small, legible, grounded set of mechanisms can recursively generate more capable cognition **before language**, and to embody those research results into a reusable substrate whenever the evidence is strong enough to touch reality.

## The two-arm convergence pattern
The project SHALL operate as a continuous **Research ↔ Reality Embodiment dual arm**, not as a serial plan of “research for a long time, then someday build the real thing.”

Research arm:
- generate hypotheses and mechanisms;
- attack claims;
- find failure boundaries;
- distinguish representation/wiring/authority/evaluator/resource defects;
- preserve UNKNOWN and counterexamples;
- strip donor ideas for mechanisms, scars, and tests without importing donor ontology wholesale.

Reality embodiment arm:
- take sufficiently earned mechanisms and make them part of a reusable, environment-neutral substrate surface;
- use real code, persisted state, restart, external environment adapters, qualification services, and ordinary action execution;
- prefer durable interfaces over one-off fixture magic;
- pressure coexistence of mechanisms over a lifetime, not only isolated unit demonstrations.

The two arms SHALL converge repeatedly.

`RESEARCH_RESULT_CAN_TOUCH_REALITY_WHEN_EARNED` does not mean `RESEARCH_RESULT_AUTO_PROMOTES_TO_CANONICAL`.

## Composition first
Before adding a new primitive, manager, registry, policy engine, ontology, or subsystem, ask:

> Which existing mechanisms already contain part of this behavior, and what exact distinction/binding is genuinely missing?

Core law:
`MISSING_BEHAVIOR != MISSING_MECHANISM`.

The desired architecture is not a cathedral of named faculties. Previously earned capabilities should increasingly become ingredients for later capabilities.

The project prefers:
- local missing distinctions;
- thin composition bridges;
- evidence/currentness guards;
- explicit external qualification boundaries;
- reuse of existing append-only/history/currentness/representation owners.

The project resists:
- new cross-cutting managers;
- hidden global state;
- semantic registries introduced because a campaign is difficult;
- harness code that quietly chooses the answer.

## Capability may emerge; authority does not
This is one of the most important constitutional rules.

A newly composed structure may become useful without thereby becoming:
- true;
- current;
- qualified;
- general;
- safe;
- executable;
- semantically meaningful.

Compact law:
`DERIVED != QUALIFIED != CURRENT != AUTHORIZED`.

Related law:
`PROPOSAL_RETURNED != ACTION_INDICATED`.

Future language generalization:
`UTTERANCE_PRODUCED != REFERENT_BOUND`.

## Grounded operational substrate before semantics
Microseed should reason from and develop around operationally grounded material such as:
- opaque state/action handles;
- observations and actual outcomes;
- evidence/provenance;
- currentness/staleness;
- qualified relations;
- operational frames/episodes;
- regulatory pressure;
- bounded hypotheses;
- UNKNOWN / DEFER states;
- counterfactual/rehearsal surfaces;
- capability contracts;
- action-outcome learning;
- evidence deficits;
- developmental history;
- operational referent signatures/classes where earned.

Human-readable names are instrumentation for researchers. They SHALL NOT silently become organism semantics, world truth, goals, object identities, regimes, or policies.

## Preserve a working theory of ignorance
The project values distinct, owned refusal states as positive architecture, not embarrassment.

Examples already earned in the lineage include:
- `SEARCH_BUDGET_EXHAUSTED_NOT_SATURATED`;
- `MULTIPLE_CURRENT_EPISTEMIC_OPPORTUNITIES`;
- `CURRENT_GENERATOR_TRANSITION_UNREPRESENTED`;
- `UNKNOWN_INCOMPLETE`;
- explicit DEFER behavior on stale/missing/ambiguous evidence.

Do not collapse these into a generic failure or “best guess.”

A system that cannot name why it does not know is not allowed to launder uncertainty into action.

## Prelingual means prelingual
Do not bolt language on because language would make demos easier.

Language is intentionally downstream of a stronger substrate threshold.

Before language, the project wants a recognizable developmental organism/substrate that can survive:
- long-lived experience;
- restart/currentness;
- drift and relearning;
- bounded endogenous program construction;
- operational persistence/reassociation;
- many-referent and partial-observability pressure;
- resource/search limits;
- ambiguity and refusal;
- lawful action execution;
- increasingly endogenous information seeking;
- operational self/body continuity pressure.

Language should emerge as another capability composition problem, not as an external semantic operating system installed above the organism.

Pre-language scars:
- `SIGNAL != REFERENCE`;
- `TOKEN_EMITTED != TOKEN_MEANS`;
- `UTTERANCE_PRODUCED != REFERENT_BOUND`;
- `LANGUAGE_QUALITY != EPISTEMIC_AUTHORITY`;
- `FLUENCY != CURRENT_PREMISE`.

## Research honesty is part of the product
The project’s value depends heavily on its refusal to claim what has not been earned.

Desired behavior:
- tool success != task success;
- direct scratch PASS != sealed milestone;
- publication != canonical promotion;
- operational reassociation != numerical identity;
- signal use != semantic reference;
- externally qualified route != self-qualified cognition;
- four-session success != general autonomy;
- finite fixture success != universal theorem.

A negative result is useful if it precisely names where the organism stopped.

Do not optimize the project for flattering AGI narratives.

The deeper research contribution is the map of:
> what the organism can originate from owned history and composition versus what still has to be supplied from outside.

## Lineage discipline
Keep these distinct at all times:
- canonical Main-Dev;
- research baseline;
- sealed/published research descendant;
- unsealed local research;
- experimental branch;
- donor/signal;
- observed result;
- inferred explanation;
- speculative future claim.

No silent promotion across those boundaries.

Current long-lived law:
`LOCAL_SEALED_RESEARCH != PUBLICATION != CANONICAL_PROMOTION`.

Additional gate:
`EXPERIMENT_MATURE != PRELINGUAL_SUBSTRATE_V1`.

## PRELINGUAL_SUBSTRATE_V1 intent
V1 is a promotion decision, not a milestone number.

The review should open only when the evidence supports at least:
- multiple independent cross-lineage compositions without a new stateful cognitive manager for the tested behavior;
- large-N currentness/dependency pressure that does not collapse into global requalification theater;
- sustained rich-world lifetime composition;
- campaign code no longer chooses the important latent structure;
- clean publication/verification lineage;
- explicit remaining assistance and authority ceilings.

Do not call V1 because “it feels substrate-like.”

## Engineering method
Use hostile engineering:

`build -> attack -> isolate failure -> strip/mutate -> embody -> attack again`.

Before adding anything, localize failure across:
- wiring;
- representation;
- binding;
- currentness/authorization;
- observability;
- execution;
- resources/search;
- evaluator/harness;
- persistence/restart.

Preserve earned failure boundaries.

Do not remove a scar merely because a successor later closes it. Record the supersession.

## Local-first runtime intent
When the server can inspect/store/execute/verify something, prefer the server over chat.

Chat is a control surface, not the main work surface.

Use server-side:
- files;
- archives;
- Git;
- tests;
- execution logs;
- checkpointing;
- state/continuity;
- long campaigns;
- bounded readback.

Avoid wasting thread life on large avoidable dumps.

## Publication discipline
For substantive research passes that earn a seal:

`VERIFY -> LOCAL SEAL -> PUSH research/ms1888-replay -> INDEPENDENT REMOTE READBACK`.

Never conflate:
- prepared to run;
- submitted;
- started;
- completed;
- passed;
- sealed;
- pushed;
- remotely verified;
- canonically promoted.

## Failure-aware execution discipline
Recent thread failures exposed control-plane failure classes. Preserve the operational fixes:
- single-flight server calls when reliability matters;
- bounded outputs;
- no giant broad job listings;
- no duplicate whole-suite launches because status is noisy;
- distinguish ghost metadata from live process reality;
- distinguish buffered/slow from stalled using process/log growth;
- freeze hashes around witnesses;
- do not mutate while a whole-suite witness is running;
- classify HTTP/transport/supervision failures separately from scientific/test failures;
- after route failure, use a simpler bounded fallback instead of repeating the same toxic request.

## Prose rule — “1991” doctrine
Use ordinary educated English around the mechanism, proper technical language for the mechanism.

Mechanism first.
Precision second.
Style third.
Vocabulary never gets to compete with understanding.

Do not dumb down real technical concepts.
Do not perform academic-vocabulary theater.

## What success looks like
Success is not “Microseed says intelligent things.”

Success is an increasingly self-consistent substrate where:
- cognition grows through grounded composition;
- useful structure can become operational without hidden semantic injection;
- ignorance remains explicit;
- authority remains traceable;
- long-lived experience survives restart without stale reauthorization;
- environmental reality can falsify the organism;
- scientific claims stay bounded to evidence;
- a fresh engineer/model can trace exactly which mechanism carried each result.

If the substrate ultimately stops short of AGI, the project should still leave a precise map of the boundary rather than an ambiguous demo stack.
