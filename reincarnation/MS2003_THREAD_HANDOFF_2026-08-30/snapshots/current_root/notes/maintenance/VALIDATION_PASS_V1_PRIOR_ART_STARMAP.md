## Strong comparators found

### 1. Baranes & Oudeyer — SAGG-RIAC / intrinsically motivated goal exploration
Paper: `arXiv:1301.4862v1` — *Active Learning of Inverse Models with Intrinsically Motivated Goal Exploration in Robots* (2013).
Abstract-level mechanism signal:
- robot actively samples novel parameterized tasks/goals;
- selection criterion is competence progress;
- selected task triggers lower-level goal-directed policy learning;
- reported faster/more efficient exploration than random or standard motor babbling;
- system can discover learnable vs unlearnable task-space regions.

Pressure on Microseed:
- autonomous selection of what to try next is clearly established prior art;
- novelty cannot rest on the bare claim "agent decides what experiment/action is worth trying";
- candidate Microseed distinction, if earned, must lie in its authority/currentness semantics, exact discriminator binding, abstention structure, or non-curiosity/non-reward decision criterion rather than autonomous exploration itself.

### 2. Oudeyer — computational theories of curiosity-driven learning
Paper: `arXiv:1802.10546v2` — *Computational Theories of Curiosity-Driven Learning* (2018).
Abstract-level mechanism signal:
- curiosity supports discovery under rare/deceptive rewards;
- normative and heuristic computational frameworks;
- learning progress / world-model learning / curriculum self-organization;
- developmental robotics as experimental substrate.

Pressure on Microseed:
- competence/learning progress and curiosity are mature comparator families;
- Microseed should explicitly state whether its epistemic pressure is equivalent to curiosity, a different regulator, or an authority-constrained composition of decision consequence + discrimination + feasibility;
- `EPISTEMIC_UNCERTAINTY != NORMATIVE_PRIORITY` has a meaningful external comparator: many intrinsic-motivation systems intentionally transform uncertainty/learning progress into selection pressure, whereas Microseed claims a narrower gate tied to current regulatory consequence.

### 3. Sajid, Da Costa, Parr & Friston — active inference / Bayesian optimal design
Paper: `arXiv:2110.04074v1` — *Active inference, Bayesian optimal design, and expected utility* (2021).
Abstract-level mechanism signal:
- expected free energy combines Bayesian decision theory and optimal Bayesian design;
- information-seeking behavior emerges naturally;
- without outcome preferences it reduces to expected information-gain maximization / optimal Bayesian design;
- expected utility, information gain, and expected free energy produce distinguishable behavior.

Pressure on Microseed:
- information-seeking action selection under decision relevance is heavily formalized prior art;
- Microseed must not claim novelty for coupling epistemic value and action selection in general;
- a plausible differentiator is that Microseed treats priority/information/feasibility/currentness as separately re-derived authority premises rather than one scalar/free-energy objective, but this requires direct full-text comparison rather than rhetoric.

### 4. Foster et al. — Bayesian optimal experimental design
Paper: `arXiv:1903.05480v3` — *Variational Bayesian Optimal Experimental Design* (2019/2020).
Abstract-level mechanism signal:
- BOED explicitly allocates limited experimental resources by maximizing expected information gain;
- practical difficulty is estimating expected information gain efficiently;
- variational/amortized methods improve design selection.

Pressure on Microseed:
- experiment-worth selection via information value is established;
- Microseed's discriminator logic should be compared against expected-information-gain design, especially where Microseed refuses to collapse current normative priority and epistemic information into one selection score.

### 5. Dette, Guchenko & Melas — Bayesian optimal discriminating designs
Paper: `arXiv:1508.00279v1` — *Efficient computation of Bayesian optimal discriminating designs* (2015).
Abstract-level mechanism signal:
- explicitly chooses experimental designs to discriminate among competing regression models;
- uses Bayesian Kullback-Leibler discrimination criteria;
- focuses on efficient computation across many model comparisons.

Pressure on Microseed:
- "choose an experiment that discriminates live alternatives" is established in experimental-design literature;
- Microseed's possible contribution is therefore not the existence of a discriminator objective, but how that objective is bounded by current operational authority, exact source ancestry, physical action closure, and abstention.
