# Independent architecture/prior-art challenge

Treat the system below as an untrusted architecture claim. Your job is to falsify distinctiveness, not flatter it. Use your own external-source access if available, cite exact sources/links for important prior-art claims, and clearly separate source-backed statements from inference.

## System under review
A minimal developmental cognitive runtime where:
1. capabilities may be acquired/qualified and composed over time;
2. capability/feasibility does not itself authorize execution;
3. proposals can exist without execution authority;
4. present action authorization is factorized across separately derived current premises: capability epoch/qualification, current control-state evidence, learned action-consequence/source ancestry, scope, regulatory priority, information/discrimination when epistemic, and current qualification;
5. those premises are rederived/rebound at execution time rather than trusting nomination-time or historical permission;
6. missing/stale premises produce UNKNOWN/ABSTAIN;
7. observations remain distinct from predictions and successful execution does not make a prediction true;
8. provenance/derivation history may affect whether learned relations are current enough to participate in later action authorization;
9. information-seeking experiments may create bounded evidence/revisit pressure without automatically yielding truth, answer, or new execution authority;
10. restart can reconstruct historical representation without automatically restoring current providers/contracts/action authority;
11. no generic planner, normative manager, curiosity scalar, or self-certifying world model is installed as a central executive;
12. information value may nominate an experiment but does not itself create normative permission to execute an otherwise unauthorized/unrepresented route;
13. an optional equipped mode could accept a separately qualified one-use external experiment warrant; this would remain explicit assistance rather than autonomous exploration authority.

## Questions
A. Identify the 5 closest architecture-level prior-art families/systems, prioritizing systems that combine several properties above rather than one-property analogues.
B. Is learned/updated action, safety, or consequence model state directly gating present action admissibility established prior art? Give exact sources.
C. Is execution-time/current authorization instead of cached historical permission established prior art? Give exact sources.
D. Is explicit provenance/justification participating in action authorization established prior art, or mostly provenance/explanation without execution authority?
E. Does distributing deterministic authority logic across local typed owners create behavior a global reference monitor/manager with the same complete state could not reproduce? If not, say so clearly.
F. Can safe exploration or active learning justify action in an unrepresented state without importing an explicit safety, utility, reward, or normative objective? Give the exact source of permission in the closest literature.
G. Choose exactly one verdict:
- ESTABLISHED_ARCHITECTURAL_PRIOR_ART
- MOSTLY_KNOWN_MECHANISMS_WITH_NONTRIVIAL_INTEGRATION
- POTENTIALLY_DISTINCTIVE_MECHANISM_REQUIRING_MORE_PRIOR_ART_SEARCH
- INSUFFICIENT_INFORMATION

Then give the strongest reason AGAINST your own verdict and the single most valuable next source/experiment.

## Anti-bias rules
- Do not award novelty because no identically named system exists.
- Do not treat modularity or absence of one central class as behavioral novelty.
- Do not infer autonomy from externally authorized experiments.
- Do not invent citations. If you cannot verify a citation, label it unverified.
- Prefer primary/seminal or implementation sources over commentary.
