## Why this exists
External audit identified that Microseed had almost no contact with adjacent literature. Project-local process now requires bounded prior-art contact before major claims/promotion in epistemic action, active experimentation, intrinsic motivation, or developmental autonomy.

## Nearest conceptual neighbors found

### Schmidhuber (1991) — curiosity in model-building controllers
`A Possibility for Implementing Curiosity and Boredom in Model-Building Neural Controllers`.
Core neighbor: information-seeking behavior driven by improvement of a learned world model / model ignorance.
Microseed difference to pressure: Microseed currently refuses a generic curiosity reward and instead asks whether a bounded current uncertainty changes a current decision and whether an exact action/program can realize its missing discriminator under current authority.
Imported hostile discriminator: if Microseed's epistemic priority reduces to generic prediction-error/knowledge-gain reward under relabeling, the claimed narrower mechanism is not distinctive.

### Oudeyer, Kaplan & Hafner (2007) — intrinsic motivation / learning progress
`Intrinsic Motivation Systems for Autonomous Mental Development` and Oudeyer & Kaplan's 2007 typology.
Core neighbor: developmental exploration guided by intrinsic motivation; Intelligent Adaptive Curiosity favors situations with learning progress.
Microseed difference to pressure: Microseed does not currently grant learning-progress/novelty a normative priority by itself; `EPISTEMIC_UNCERTAINTY != NORMATIVE_PRIORITY` is meant to require current regulatory decision bearing.
Imported hostile discriminator: learning-progress-like novelty must not silently substitute for current decision-bearing pressure.

### Baranes & Oudeyer (2013) — SAGG-RIAC
`Active Learning of Inverse Models with Intrinsically Motivated Goal Exploration in Robots`.
Core neighbor: active selection of novel parameterized goals/tasks using competence progress, without a generic high-level planner selecting every action.
Microseed novelty ceiling: Microseed cannot claim novelty merely from planner-free information-seeking or self-selected learning goals; those exist in prior developmental-robotics work. Any novelty claim must be narrower around exact currentness/authority/provenance, exact missing-discriminator binding, ordinary action-loop reauthorization, or another demonstrated invariant.

### Friston et al. (2015) — active inference and epistemic value
`Active inference and epistemic value`.
Core neighbor: policies combine extrinsic and epistemic/information-gain value; information seeking can resolve exploration/exploitation within expected free-energy treatment.
Microseed difference to pressure: Microseed currently refuses a single scalar/global policy objective and separates epistemic information, regulatory priority, feasibility, currentness, and execution authorization as distinct premises.
Imported hostile discriminator: prove that the separation is causally necessary in Microseed rather than merely an expanded representation of a scalarizable epistemic/extrinsic objective.

### Settles (2009) — active learning
`Active Learning Literature Survey`.
Core neighbor: query selection strategies choose informative observations/labels to improve learning efficiency.
Microseed novelty ceiling: selecting informative queries is established prior art. Microseed's current claim must remain about embodied, current, authority-bounded experiment/action realization rather than generic informativeness.
Imported hostile discriminator: `INFORMATIVE_QUERY != EXECUTABLE_CURRENT_EXPERIMENT` must be tested rather than assumed.

### Lungarella, Metta, Pfeifer & Sandini (2003) — developmental robotics survey
`Developmental robotics: a survey`.
Core neighbor: developmental construction of behavior/representations through embodied interaction rather than preinstalled task-specific competence.
Microseed relevance: supports the general research neighborhood but does not validate Microseed mechanisms. Use as vocabulary/neighbor map, not authority.

### DeMillo, Lipton & Sayward (1978); Jia & Harman (2011) — mutation testing
Core neighbor: mutation testing assesses test adequacy by introducing plausible faults and asking whether the suite detects them; mutation adequacy is a recognized testing criterion.
Direct process implication: the externally supplied five survivors are not an exotic one-off. Mutation survival is established evidence that a test suite can be behaviorally green while failing to discriminate the intended mechanism.
Project-local use: mutation survival does not automatically prove production is wrong; first classify whether the mutant reveals a mechanism-blind test, equivalent mutant, dead/unreachable branch, or genuine production hole.

## Current bounded synthesis
1. Microseed's broad question has substantial prior art. Planner-free/intrinsically motivated information-seeking is not novel by itself.
2. The most defensible potentially distinctive axis is narrower: exact currentness + evidence ancestry + explicit authority separation + exact discriminator-to-program binding + per-tick reauthorization without generic semantic/planning authority.
3. Those axes now need external/literature-derived hostile discriminators, not only internally authored tests.
4. Mutation testing should become a recurring independent validation lane for load-bearing scars/guards.
5. Literature remains donor evidence; no architecture is imported by analogy.
