# Research ↔ Reality Embodiment Dual-Arm Convergence — Restored Active Doctrine

Date: 2026-08-29 ET
Scope: ProtoAGI Microseed research process; does not alter organism architecture or canonical Main-Dev by itself.

## Correction
The active Microseed process SHALL NOT be treated as a serial pipeline of:
`research everything -> later build substrate -> later touch reality`.

The intended metabolism is the already-earned Rahl/Microseed pattern:
`PROBE -> DERIVE -> VERIFY -> EMBODY -> RECURSE`,
operating as two converging arms:

### Research arm
- expose a discriminator;
- derive the smallest candidate mechanism/invariant;
- pressure alternatives and wrong-paths;
- preserve assistance ancestry and authority boundaries.

### Reality-embodiment arm
- force the research result against the current organism and external/independent environment surfaces as soon as it is lawful to do so;
- if existing organism mechanisms already realize the research result, embody the result as regression/whole-organism evidence and do not add production machinery;
- if a genuinely missing mechanism is demonstrated, implement the minimum sufficient mechanism and immediately retest it against whole-organism reality;
- if reality falsifies the research interpretation, demote/reopen the research claim rather than patching tests to narrative.

The two arms SHALL repeatedly converge, not wait for one another to finish globally.

## Governing laws
- `RESEARCH_RESULT != REALITY_EMBODIMENT`.
- `REALITY_FAILURE != AUTOMATIC_MISSING_MECHANISM`.
- `MISSING_BEHAVIOR != MISSING_MECHANISM`.
- `CONSTITUENT_PASS != WHOLE_ORGANISM_PASS`.
- `EMBODIMENT != PRODUCTION_MUTATION`; lawful composition with zero source delta is a valid embodiment result.
- `SUBSTRATEIZATION != POST-RESEARCH CLEANUP`; substrate pressure is itself a research instrument.
- unchanged bytes need not be ceremonially rebuilt, but every newly claimed integrated property needs current whole-organism/reality evidence.

## Accelerated campaign flow
When no blocker exists, the lab SHOULD run multiple bounded campaigns in one active turn/session:
1. close the current discriminator;
2. embody/pressure it immediately;
3. seal only after current verification;
4. recurse directly into the next highest-information unblocked frontier.

Do not create artificial one-campaign-per-turn pacing.
Stop only on:
- a real authority/governance blocker;
- missing external evidence that cannot be generated locally;
- a scientific contradiction needing re-framing;
- a production mutation whose safety/authority boundary is unresolved;
- or actual tool/environment failure.

## Substrate implication
The honest substrate effort SHALL run as a parallel reality arm now. Campaigns should increasingly prefer:
- reusable environment-neutral adapters over one-off fixtures;
- long-lived restartable development over short single-purpose worlds;
- independent evidence/qualification services over campaign-local truth injection;
- multiple environment families;
- cold-start developmental acquisition;
- exact whole-organism readback.

This does not license premature abstraction. Build substrate surfaces only when repeated campaign evidence shows a stable cross-environment owner/interface.
