## Amendment
The fixed five-Ranger ceiling from the current V3 package is overridden for this project.

OARR may continue for as many Rangers/passes as remain materially discriminating and Pareto-useful.

The governing stop condition is not a fixed Ranger count. Stop or switch when one or more of the following becomes true:
- marginal information gain is low relative to cost;
- the route enters clear diminishing returns;
- additional Rangers mostly repeat the same evidence lineage;
- the surviving hypothesis set is no longer being meaningfully narrowed;
- the branch is polishing rather than discriminating;
- a sibling frontier has higher expected information gain / lower cost;
- the current route is stalled, harness-limited, or otherwise non-informative;
- a hard boundary or lawful UNKNOWN has been reached.

Continue when additional Rangers still have materially different failure surfaces, rivals, assumptions, or authority boundaries to attack and the expected information gain remains competitive.
