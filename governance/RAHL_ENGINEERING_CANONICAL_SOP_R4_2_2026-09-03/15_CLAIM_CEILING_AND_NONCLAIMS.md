# 15 — Claim Ceiling and Nonclaims

This package claims only to be a verified, project-agnostic in-house process/cold-start SOP under its stated verifier and hostile-test assurance ceiling.

It does **not** claim:
- flawless engineering or elimination of human/model blind spots;
- that base-tier machinery guarantees correctness merely by being invoked;
- that proportional use requires visible ritual or identical choreography across tasks;
- universal truth of every heuristic;
- domain/product/architecture authority;
- legal or regulatory sufficiency;
- semantic equivalence to every sentence in all ancestry;
- that carrying ancestry makes ancestry current;
- that a continuity artifact is current-state proof;
- that RES synthesis creates doctrine;
- that a green verifier proves unknown-failure robustness;
- that one environment receipt seals every relevant environmental dimension;
- that all projects should use identical tools, languages, campaign lengths, archive part sizes, or runtime topology;
- that a fresh chat is a fresh blind evaluator when memory/history/retrieval is shared.

Assurance classes in this release SHALL remain explicit and demotable under better evidence.
- that a machine verifier, test suite, manifest, or hostile mutation suite substitutes for complete semantic reading when the semantic gate applies;
- that local-first execution forbids interactive control-plane work when interaction is genuinely required;
- that every finite task must use the same launcher/job implementation; the obligation is to choose the strongest practical execution plane and preserve readback.

- that operator-side execution is always faster; it is the default locality/ownership preference when available and sufficient;
- that assistant/model-side execution is forbidden; it remains lawful when materially better or faster for the specific step;
- that execution-plane fallback permits bypassing safety, approval, access-control, or authority boundaries.
