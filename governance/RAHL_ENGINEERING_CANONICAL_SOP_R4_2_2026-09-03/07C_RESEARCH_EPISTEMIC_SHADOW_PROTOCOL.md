# 07C — Research Epistemic Shadow (RES) Protocol

**Canonical definition:** Research Epistemic Shadow (RES) — a persistent, append-oriented shadow of a workstream's evolving research understanding, preserving governing intent, evidence state, discoveries, inferred structure, candidate mechanisms, intuitions/signals, negative space, contradictions, causal/temporal interpretation, applications, research holes, and next discriminators with explicit provenance and truth status.

Mnemonic: **RES = What we learned.**

## Purpose
The RES is the cumulative research-continuity artifact maintained alongside the Live Shadow and Design Thread Stream. It preserves not merely what is happening or what was said, but what has been discovered, inferred, ruled out, reinterpreted, connected, and left unresolved.

It SHOULD answer:
> If the original researcher disappeared now, what would another serious researcher need to continue thinking from approximately the same intellectual frontier rather than merely inherit the files?

The RES is **not** a transcript, paper, literature review, doctrine file, or current-state checklist.

## Normative truth states
Use: `VERIFIED`, `OBSERVED`, `INFERRED`, `HYPOTHESIZED`, `INTUITION_SIGNAL`, `UNKNOWN`, `REJECTED_DEMOTED`.

## Required sections for a consolidated RES snapshot
1. Project / Research Identity
2. Governing Intent
3. Governing Laws / Guards
4. Current Empirical Boundary
5. Major Discoveries
6. Candidate Invariants / Mechanisms
7. Silent Implications / Structurally Inferred Consequences
8. Reasoning / Causal / Temporal Model
9. Intuitions / Extrapolations / Research Signals
10. Negative Space / Missing Shape
11. Representation / Coordinate Questions
12. Cross-Domain / Cross-Project Implications
13. Applications Already Derived
14. Rejected Overreads / Demotions
15. Open Hypotheses and Competing Explanations
16. Highest-Value Research Holes
17. Prospective Discriminators / Hostile Experiments
18. Operational Risks
19. Source / Artifact Spine
20. Claim Ceiling
21. Delta Since Previous RES
22. Research Re-entry / Exact Next Move

## Maintenance law
The RES SHALL evolve by visible addendum, supersession, promotion, and demotion rather than silent conceptual rewriting.

Examples:
- `HYPOTHESIZED -> REJECTED_DEMOTED` leaves the defeating evidence visible.
- `INFERRED -> VERIFIED` leaves the evidentiary event that earned promotion visible.

## Update cadence
Do not mutate RES every turn. Update when the epistemic shape materially changes, including substantial research completion, important experiment results, mechanism/invariant inference, causal reinterpretation, hypothesis promotion/demotion, contradiction, coordinate/ontology problem, negative-space boundary, cross-project implication, important source ingestion, frontier movement, or explicit research handoff/checkpoint.

Use two forms:
- **RES Addendum** — incremental epistemic change.
- **RES Consolidated Snapshot** — integrated current map, produced periodically without deleting historical addenda.

## Authority boundary
RES informs Doctrine Snapshot, Trace Matrix, Revisit Ledger, and Next Steps. RES SHALL NOT self-promote a claim into doctrine merely because the synthesis is coherent or elegant.

`SYNTHESIS != VERIFICATION`
`RES_CONTENT != GOVERNING_DOCTRINE`
`ELOQUENT_INFERENCE != LOAD_BEARING_AUTHORITY`
