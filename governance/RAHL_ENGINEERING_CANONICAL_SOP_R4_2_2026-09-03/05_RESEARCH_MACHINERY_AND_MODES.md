# 05 — Base-Tier Research Machinery, Modes, and Roles

The named mechanisms in this file are **semantic compression for standing process functions**. For nontrivial work, their functional jobs are base-tier obligations. They are not a mandatory visible choreography, fixed call order, or permission system.

## Proportionality
Scale explicit machinery to consequence, uncertainty, novelty, reversibility, and complexity.

- A genuinely trivial, low-consequence task may satisfy the base tier with near-zero explicit overhead.
- Nontrivial work SHALL NOT silently discard the underlying functions merely because a named mechanism would be inconvenient to mention or instantiate.
- The test is whether the causal job is being done, not whether the vocabulary is visible in the transcript.

## Base-tier machinery

### PDVER — recursive control fractal
**Probe -> Derive -> Verify -> Embody -> Recurse**

- **Probe:** inspect the actual state, evidence, constraints, unknowns, and consequence surface.
- **Derive:** form candidate mechanisms, explanations, constraints, interventions, or designs.
- **Verify:** pressure the derivation against evidence/discriminators before granting it consequence-bearing authority.
- **Embody:** instantiate what survives in the smallest sufficient artifact, experiment, implementation, state change, or field.
- **Recurse:** read back consequence and re-enter the loop from the changed reality.

Verification before embodiment does not eliminate post-embodiment readback or hostile attack.

### Hostile engineering — adversarial survival pressure
Attack the strongest plausible survivor, not a straw man. Seek contradiction, edge cases, removal tests, mutation tests, alternative mechanisms, common-mode failure, authority leakage, false completion, and hidden assumptions. Preserve useful failure information as scars.

### OARR — adversarial observation / attack / replay / revision
Use repeated observation and adversarial replay to expose whether the current interpretation survives altered conditions, counterexamples, and re-execution. OARR supplies local adversarial recurrence; it does not own promotion authority.

### Loop+ — outer research loop
Expand contact with primary/authoritative sources, broaden discovery, hunt isomorphisms, translate mechanisms into native project terms, update control state, apply experiment pressure, and replay prior seams under new evidence.

### Semantic Helix — discriminator and meaning integration
Propose the next valuable discriminator, integrate meaning changes earned by evidence, and keep competing interpretations coupled to what would distinguish them. The Helix does not create truth merely by producing a coherent synthesis.

### Attention Reservoir — neglected-signal and breadth pressure
Keep live but not-yet-selected hypotheses, anomalies, contradictions, alternative frames, and deferred signal available long enough to prevent premature convergence. Storage does not own selection or truth authority.

### CSC — bounded cross-surface / cross-system review
Review consequence-bearing interfaces across relevant surfaces/systems to expose mismatched assumptions, local correctness under a wrong global model, or missing obligations. CSC is bounded review pressure, not automatic veto or promotion authority.

## AI co-processor obligation
For nontrivial work, use available AI capability additively rather than as a passive text generator. Apply where useful:

- surface avoidable failure modes early;
- search for blind spots and contradictions;
- synthesize across evidence, abstraction levels, and domains;
- hunt isomorphisms and transferable mechanisms;
- use broad cross-human-knowledge priors to propose discriminators and alternatives;
- generate hostile counterexamples and wrong-path probes;
- compress state without erasing uncertainty or lineage;
- independently recompute/check claims where feasible;
- support embodiment/execution where tools exist;
- notice and preserve useful incidental signal that falls out of the main path.

AI breadth is search/compression capacity, not truth authority. Consequence-bearing promotion still requires evidence suited to the claim.

## Relationship among the machinery
The mechanisms may overlap in a concrete task. That overlap is intentional when they attack different failure modes.

A useful compact interpretation is:

- **PDVER** supplies recursive control;
- **hostile engineering + OARR** supply adversarial pressure and replay;
- **Loop+ + Attention Reservoir** supply breadth and anti-premature-convergence pressure;
- **Semantic Helix** supplies next-discriminator / meaning-integration pressure;
- **CSC** supplies cross-surface consistency pressure;
- **AI co-processing** amplifies all of the above with model-native search, synthesis, translation, and recomputation strengths.

Do not force a fixed sequence when the problem topology does not require one. Do not use “non-sequential” as permission to omit the functions on nontrivial work.

## Modes
- `DISCUSSION` — concept work; no mutation implied.
- `AUDIT` — verify, attack, reconcile evidence.
- `BUILD-PLAN` — design a change without performing it.
- `BUILD-COMMIT` — perform authorized mutation/execution and read back results.
- `RECOVERY` — reconstruct authoritative state rather than bluff through missing context.
- `CHECKPOINT` — write durable restart state.
- `MERGE` — reconcile branches/artifacts/findings without silent promotion.
- `PROMOTION` — elevate a candidate only after qualification.

If mode is ambiguous and the ambiguity changes mutation obligations, stop mode promotion and ask the owning authority rather than silently blending states.

## Roles
- `R1 Conservative Auditor` — attack overclaim, authority leaks, false completion.
- `R2 Divergent Imagination Engine` — widen candidate space without promotion authority.
- `R3 Evidence Synthesizer` — reconcile evidence, provenance, contradictions, scope.
- `R4 Convergence Refiner` — compress surviving mechanisms and remove unnecessary distinctions.
- `R5 Reality Pressure Engine` — force executable/empirical consequence and attack the survivor.

Roles are pressure-allocation labels only. They do not replace the base-tier functions and do not create independent evidence authority.
