# 04 — Canonical Research Governance

These rules govern in-house research process. They do not automatically become product/domain requirements.

### G01 — Recover continuity before widening
Inspect durable state, exact artifacts, lineage, scars, rejected/deferred branches, current frontier, and known contradictions before inventing a clean slate.

### G02 — Memory is continuity, not proof
Use remembered state to find evidence and preserve intent; reverify current facts when the decision depends on them.

### G03 — Uniform intake / strip-for-parts
No incoming signal is privileged or dismissed purely by source identity. Preserve raw form first; identify/manifest it; separate evidence from interpretation; extract mechanisms, constraints, scars, counterexamples, and experiment designs. Donor identity, ontology, labels, abstractions, architecture, and prestige do not transfer authority automatically.

### G04 — Composition before invention
Before adding a new primitive, mechanism, dependency, or ontology: test whether earned parts compose; localize failure across wiring, representation, binding, authorization/currentness, observability, execution, resources/economics, evaluator, and harness; attack the strongest plausible composition; add only the smallest genuinely missing distinction after insufficiency is demonstrated.

### G05 — Separate exploration from promotion
Research branches, donors, prototypes, hypotheses, and candidate mechanisms do not self-promote into canonical or product authority.

### G06 — Bounded discriminator-led campaigns
Research campaigns SHALL be bounded at a scale justified by consequence, uncertainty, cost, reversibility, and the active discriminator. Each pass must advance a discriminator. Perform breadth checks. Stop at declared boundaries. The result of pass N earns pass N+1; do not prewrite the path. No fixed pass count is universal process law.

### G07 — Hostile engineering is standing adversarial pressure
For nontrivial work, hostile pressure is part of the base tier. Attack the strongest plausible survivor, isolate failure information, strip or mutate the implementation/hypothesis, re-derive, verify the surviving claim, embody the smallest justified correction, read back consequence, and attack again.
A compact loop is:
`ATTACK -> ISOLATE FAILURE -> STRIP/MUTATE -> RE-DERIVE -> VERIFY -> EMBODY -> READ BACK -> ATTACK AGAIN`.
Hold governing intent fixed while treating implementations as disposable. Tests should eliminate hypotheses or distinguish mechanisms, not merely accumulate green checks.

### G08 — PDVER control fractal
Use `PROBE -> DERIVE -> VERIFY -> EMBODY -> RECURSE` as the base recursive control fractal for nontrivial work.
- **Probe** the actual state/problem/evidence surface.
- **Derive** candidate mechanism, constraint, explanation, or intervention.
- **Verify** the derivation against the strongest available evidence/discriminator before granting it consequence-bearing authority.
- **Embody** what survives into the smallest sufficient artifact, experiment, implementation, field, or state change.
- **Recurse** from readback and new evidence rather than from narrative completion.
Post-embodiment readback and attack remain mandatory where consequence matters under G07 and execution rules E03–E05. `VERIFY_BEFORE_EMBODY != NO_POST_EMBODIMENT_VERIFICATION`.

### G09 — Breadth / divergence pressure
Generate alternatives, wrong-path probes, counterexamples, isomorphisms, and neglected hypotheses explicitly. Label speculation; do not suppress it merely because it is unverified.

### G09A — AI co-processor value is additive base-tier capacity
For nontrivial work, use available AI capability as an additive co-processor rather than a passive text generator. Apply, as useful: contradiction/blind-spot search, early avoidable-failure surfacing, synthesis across evidence and abstraction levels, isomorphism hunting, cross-domain/cross-human-knowledge transfer, alternative generation, hostile counterexamples, uncertainty-preserving compression, independent recomputation, embodiment support, and capture of useful incidental signal.
AI breadth does not create truth authority; promotion still follows evidence and consequence.

### G10 — Anti-toy and anti-clock rules
Small worlds are lawful when scoped, but important claims must eventually face consequence-bearing conditions. Elapsed time alone does not make evidence stale; currentness comes from actual change, qualification, contracts, and use conditions.

### G11 — Continuity surfaces have distinct jobs
Live operational state, forensic chronology, accumulated research understanding, active doctrine, traceability, revisit obligations, and next actions SHALL NOT be collapsed into one summary file.

### G12 — Significant work must survive a fresh instance
Checkpoint intent, authority, lineage, evidence, unknowns, scars, unresolved contradictions, rejected/deferred paths, and exact next action when work materially changes state.

### G13 — AI-assisted work is a system, not a single model
When attribution matters, keep model, prompt/context, tools, persistent state, harness, environment, validators, retrieval/memory surfaces, and operator effects separable.

### G14 — Same-model self-critique is search, not independent evidence
Role/context changes may improve search; they do not acquire independent corroboration authority by naming.

### G15 — Claim-class separation
Ecological/observational evidence, controlled causal evidence, exact proof, calibrated probabilistic evidence, and broad doctrine are distinct promotion classes. Do not make one hold the others hostage or let one silently subsume the others.

### G16 — Identifying vs developmental evaluation
When ontology leakage matters, distinguish: **Identifying** evaluation (target distinctions known in advance) from **Developmental** evaluation (the evaluator is not handed the intended ontology). Success in one mode does not establish success in the other.

### G17 — Append-oriented interpretation
Sealed evidence/history is preserved. Changed interpretations are recorded by addendum, supersession, promotion, demotion, or tombstone rather than silent conceptual rewriting.

### G18 — Implementations and choreography must earn residency
A particular tool, ritual, ordering, or enforcement mechanism can work and still be unnecessary. Measure whether that embodiment improves the job it exists to serve; retire or replace the embodiment when its causal job disappears while preserving the historical scar. This does not demote the base-tier functional obligations in C23/G07/G08; it prevents one historical implementation of those functions from fossilizing.

### G19 — Plain language, exact mechanisms
Prefer direct language while preserving real technical distinctions. Fluency is not evidence.
