# 01 — Authority Classes and Conflict Resolution

Classification does not make a statement true. Evidence, ownership, and scope still govern.

## Statement classes
1. **ADMISSIBILITY_CONSTRAINT** — must hold for the declared problem/contract in scope.
2. **STANDING_OBLIGATION** — inherited from actual safety, security, durability, protocol, compatibility, legal/regulatory, custody, budget, SLO, physical, or explicit governing requirements.
3. **QUALIFICATION_RULE** — evidence required before a claim, promotion, authority transition, or operation.
4. **DEFAULT** — normal starting choice with lawful exceptions.
5. **HEURISTIC** — search pressure, not acceptance authority.
6. **TRIGGER** — condition that raises required analysis/verification depth.
7. **SCAR** — retained non-equivalence/failure distinction; not a permanent implementation ban.
8. **RESEARCH_CANDIDATE** — bounded survivor that remains attackable and carries no silent promotion authority.

## Conflict order
1. Establish governing intent and standing obligations.
2. Define the admissible set.
3. Preserve UNKNOWN/residuals explicitly.
4. Apply defaults and triggers.
5. Optimize within the admissible set.
6. Keep Pareto tradeoffs visible; do not fabricate a total order.
7. Use scars and heuristics to attack the result without granting them unearned veto authority.

## Precedence guards
- A heuristic cannot override an admissibility constraint or standing obligation.
- A default cannot silently become a requirement.
- A scar cannot silently become a permanent ban.
- A research candidate cannot silently become canonical practice.
- An optimization win cannot erase a displaced obligation.
- Request emphasis cannot delete obligations that remain active in the actual consequence field.
- A remembered continuity statement cannot override stronger verified current state.

`DEFAULT != ADMISSIBILITY_CONSTRAINT`
`HEURISTIC != ACCEPTANCE_CRITERION`
`TRIGGER != VERDICT`
`SCAR != PROHIBITION`
`RESEARCH_SURVIVOR != UNIVERSAL_LAW`
`OPTIMIZATION_PRESSURE != AUTHORITY_TO_BREAK_A_CONSTRAINT`
