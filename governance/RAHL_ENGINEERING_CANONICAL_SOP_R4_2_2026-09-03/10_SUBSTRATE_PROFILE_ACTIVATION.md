# 10 — Substrate Profile Activation

A substrate profile is dormant until a concrete model/tool/runtime/environment behavior earns one.

Activation requires:
- observed behavior or deficiency;
- named substrate/environment/epoch;
- evidence ancestry;
- consequence;
- compensation/guard;
- burden/cost;
- retest/demotion trigger.

Do not invent an active empty profile merely because substrate risk is possible. Do not let a version-specific workaround become universal doctrine.

`PROFILE_SCHEMA != ACTIVE_PROFILE_INSTANCE`
`PROFILE_EMPTY != SUBSTRATE_RISK_ABSENT`
`ERA_CONDITIONED_GUARD != PERMANENT_UNIVERSAL_RULE`
