# Microseed Main-Dev Operating Profile v1.0

This is an additive refinement of v0.9, not a replacement methodology. The core historical topology remains HSP → Loop+/Research → OARR → PDVER → Research/Embodiment → evidence/scars → Semantic Helix → Attention Reservoir → Main-Dev/recurse.

## MS1028–1052 additions

Structural rewrite no longer counts as a threat to developmental continuity merely because state bytes, capability inventory or recruitment topology changed. Main-Dev may use **branch-relative causal developmental lineage** as a bounded operational continuity relation. That relation has an explicit ceiling: it is not numerical selfhood, semantic self identity, execution uniqueness or exclusive successor authority.

A `SAME_BIOGRAPHY_STATE` relation must be interpreted as graph-state equivalence with copy ambiguity. Perfect copies and deterministic duplicate replay are a first-class hostile case. A copied content-bound ledger establishes possession of lineage data; it does not establish that one execution is the unique causal embodiment of that history.

Exclusive canonical successor selection may be provided by an external serialized authority, but that authority must remain explicit assistance and its availability/abstention economics must be reported. Microseed's internal biography may not self-issue it.

Merge ancestry may establish descent from multiple branches without resolving one unique numerical identity. Integrity failure forces UNKNOWN. Restoring an earlier visible state never rewinds developmental history.

## Portfolio consequence

The selected next pressure is cross-family, not another identity-only local Helix: distinguish branch-relative self-continuation, causally related sibling branch and unrelated acting entity without semantic actor labels, while preserving independent goal/feasibility/authority boundaries. This reactivates the prelingual social/other-agent audit and Grand P5 decentralized-goal pressure.

Language remains deferred. Persistent numerical selfhood remains unqualified. MS1053 is not started at this integration boundary.


## MS1053–1077 method delta

The historical method shape is unchanged. New explicit guards:

- `BEHAVIORAL_SYNCHRONY != CAUSAL_AUTHORSHIP`; passive covariance cannot establish agency relation where intervention can discriminate.
- `OPERATIONAL_COUNTERPARTY_RELATION != SEMANTIC_OR_NUMERICAL_AGENT_IDENTITY`.
- Distributed capability qualification must carry independently changing counterparty currentness when another actor is load-bearing.
- `PARENT_PRESSURE != CHILD_VALUE_STATE`; decentralized feasibility/refusal remains a real authority boundary.
- Partner-combination search cost is first-class economics; identifiability does not license exponential search.
- Social/other-agent work remains prelingual. Human role labels in source/reporting are inspection vocabulary, not cognitive tokens.
