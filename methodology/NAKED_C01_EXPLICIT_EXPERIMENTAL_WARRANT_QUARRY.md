# NAKED-C01 — Explicit Experimental Warrant Quarry

## Question
What must an A1-style constitutional experimental warrant bind so that it can authorize one exact first unmodeled physical sample without becoming curiosity, generic exploration, or general action authority?

## Status
RESEARCH-ONLY constitutional quarry. No `microseed/**` mutation. The branch's inherited abstention law remains canonical.

## Candidate under pressure
A NEW constitutional premise may derive a one-shot warrant only for one represented/current/qualified capability whose consequence is not yet modeled. The warrant binds exact capability id, epoch, signature, operational scope, issue-state digest, invocation maximum=1, and its own currentness. Ordinary capability authority remains independently required.

Information value, regulatory pressure, linguistic statements, and generic utility are deliberately absent from the derivation.

## Hostiles
Repeat use, capability substitution, epoch drift, signature drift, scope drift, issue-state drift, capability staleness, qualification loss, missing ordinary capability authority, already-modeled consequence, no warrant, and stale warrant must all abstain.

## Interpretation ceiling
A PASS would show only that an A1 warrant **can be structurally bounded** so it does not automatically become general action authority. It does not justify the constitutional premise itself and does not prove downstream physical risk is bounded.

Next pressure must attack the issuance rule and consumption semantics, especially self-authorization loops and crash/restart windows.
