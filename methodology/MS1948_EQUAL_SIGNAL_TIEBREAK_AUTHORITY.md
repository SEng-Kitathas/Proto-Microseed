## Result

`DETERMINISTIC_SEQUENCE_ID_TIEBREAK_IS_OPERATIONAL_ARBITRATION_AMONG_EQUAL_MODELED_OPTIONS_NOT_LEARNED_SIGNAL_PREFERENCE`.

- semantic preference;
- token meaning;
- convention identity;
