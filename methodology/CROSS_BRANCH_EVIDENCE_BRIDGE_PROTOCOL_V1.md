# Cross-Branch Evidence Bridge Protocol V1

## Purpose
NAKED authority research and grounded language/reference research are strongly isolated by default. Isolation is not an embargo on evidence. If one branch discovers a mechanism, falsifier, invariant, or negative result that may materially pressure the other, that signal MAY be offered across the boundary as a **bridge evidence packet**.

## Transfer rule
A bridge packet MUST carry:
- source branch and exact Git commit;
- source campaign and evidence/test pointers;
- the narrow claim being offered;
- verified/provisional/speculative label;
- authority ceiling;
- assumptions required for the claim;
- explicit nonclaims;
- reason it may matter to the receiving branch.

## Admission rule
Transfer is not adoption. The receiving branch MUST independently decide whether the signal is relevant, re-derive it in local ontology, and hostile-test it before any mechanism, premise, or doctrine becomes branch-local.

## Forbidden shortcuts
- no cherry-pick merely because code is convenient;
- no authority transfer by analogy;
- no language-derived statement may grant NAKED physical permission;
- no NAKED constitutional permission may confer reference/semantic authority;
- no shared hidden manager/state may be introduced to coordinate the branches;
- no bridge packet self-promotes either branch or canonical V1.

## Core law
`CROSS_BRANCH_SIGNAL_TRANSFER != CROSS_BRANCH_AUTHORITY_TRANSFER`.

The default remains isolation. Bridge use is exceptional, explicit, provenance-bound, and campaign-adjudicated.
