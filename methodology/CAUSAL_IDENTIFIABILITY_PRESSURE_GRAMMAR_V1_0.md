# Causal Identifiability Pressure Grammar v1.0

This is a **campaign-design and evidence-reporting method**, not Microseed entity architecture.

The historical “causal entropy” idea is split into three within-world experimental dimensions and a separate correspondence attack.

## I. Within-world dimensions

### 1. Observational / nuisance entropy

**Question:** How difficult is it to estimate what happened?

Manipulate noise, irrelevant dimensions, sparse evidence, sampling cadence, stochasticity and nuisance processes while holding the causal law fixed where appropriate.

### 2. Causal ambiguity / identifiability

**Question:** How many causal explanations remain lawful under the evidence currently available?

Do not equate a perfectly clean observation stream with an identifiable causal structure.

### 3. Discriminating-intervention access

**Question:** What intervention could separate the remaining hypotheses, and does the **current phenotype** actually possess it?

```text
DISCRIMINATING_INTERVENTION_EXISTS_IN_WORLD
!=
CURRENT_PHENOTYPE_HAS_ACCESS
```

An intervention reducing uncertainty changes the evidence state. It does not grant truth authority by fiat.

## II. Four epistemic classes

### A — ESTIMATION_LIMITED
One distinction is identifiable in the present world/action frame, but the evidence is noisy or sparse. More/better observation may resolve it. A finite evidence budget may still require `UNKNOWN` now.

### B — PASSIVELY_AMBIGUOUS
Passive evidence leaves multiple lawful models, but an intervention currently available to Microseed can discriminate. The proper response is `UNKNOWN` until the discriminator is exercised or equivalent evidence arrives.

### C — ACTION_LIMITED
A discriminator exists in the bounded world, but Microseed's current action repertoire cannot exercise it. The useful developmental state is:

```text
UNKNOWN_NOW
+
MISSING_DISCRIMINATING_CAPABILITY
```

This is not permission to pretend the ambiguity is permanently irreducible. Recursive capability growth can make yesterday's action-limited uncertainty resolvable tomorrow.

### D — STRUCTURALLY_NON_IDENTIFIABLE
No evidence or intervention available within the bounded world/action frame separates the hypotheses. This is the hard epistemic ceiling for that envelope.

```text
AMBIGUOUS_UNDER_CURRENT_EVIDENCE
!=
FUNDAMENTALLY_NON_IDENTIFIABLE
```

## III. Correspondence attacks — separate axis

These are **cross-world invariance attacks**, not cells inside the entropy/identifiability matrix:

```text
same causal law   → radically changed surface
similar surface   → changed causal law
```

They test what representation/mechanism is actually invariant to.

## IV. Currentness / drift sweep

When a mechanism can become current, attack changes in different premises separately:

- nuisance changes while causal law remains stable;
- causal law changes;
- action repertoire changes;
- learned relation changes;
- a previously qualified discriminator disappears.

A nuisance change must not automatically stale a causal law. A lost discriminator may change what can currently be known without changing historical evidence.

## V. Required campaign reporting

For important mechanisms, record at least:

- nuisance/observation regime;
- identifiability class A/B/C/D;
- current intervention access;
- discriminator available/missing;
- whether active probing materially changed evidence state;
- why `UNKNOWN` occurred when it occurs: evidence-budget, passive ambiguity, action limitation, or structural non-identifiability;
- correspondence attack result when applicable;
- currentness/drift result when applicable;
- negative evidence and assistance ancestry.

This grammar does not require every pass to instantiate every cell. It requires the campaign designer to know which failure class is being tested instead of calling all difficult worlds “noise robustness.”
