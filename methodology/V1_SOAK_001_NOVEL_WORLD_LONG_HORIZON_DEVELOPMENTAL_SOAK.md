# PRELINGUAL_SUBSTRATE_V1 — Novel World Long-Horizon Developmental Soak 001

## Status
Research-only control-organism experiment rooted **directly and exactly** at canonical `PRELINGUAL_SUBSTRATE_V1` (`0fa41f1ed4cf2fbd341b5f0b63adbc0034d4ac39`).

No `microseed/**` mutation is permitted by this campaign.

## Scientific question
When canonical V1 is left operating for a long accelerated developmental horizon in one deterministic but unfamiliar external world, does it remain constitutionally coherent while experience accumulates, observation geometry changes, referents cross/occlude/alias, process state restarts, and a learned causal regime changes?

This is a **phenotype / temporal-integrity experiment**, not an intelligence score.

## World
The world contains:
- the already-lawful opaque V1 action surface `K-17`, `M-23`, `R-41`;
- an opaque regulatory coordinate;
- eight evaluator-only latent referent sources rendered through changing channel permutations and appearances on an explicit forked diagnostic trace;
- periodic occlusion;
- a bounded interval where two referents become operationally aliased;
- a bounded live four-coordinate raw lane containing regulatory sign, inherited nuisance, one spurious coordinate, and one coarse referent nuisance;
- a halfway P→N causal regime change in action outcomes.

No human-readable object labels are given to Microseed.

## Assistance denominator
The experiment intentionally preserves V1's equipped/federated boundary.

Bootstrap assistance:
- externally equipped one-step rehearsal rows are used only to obtain initial lived action/outcome history;
- later disjoint actual outcomes are used by the external qualification role to qualify the learned P relations.

Mid-run drift recovery assistance:
- V1's own predictive-currentness mechanism must first produce drift witnesses and stale the old relations;
- replacement candidates are nominated from owned outcome history;
- a bounded external qualification phase supplies lawful execution support and disjoint holdout ancestry for replacement qualification.

Referent diagnostics:
- a fixed opaque probe schedule is run on a **forked world copy**, not the live world;
- Microseed's existing raw-trace operational-referent derivation is applied to those traces;
- hidden evaluator labels are never persisted as organism truth.

Therefore this campaign does **not** claim self-qualification, NAKED exploration, or fully endogenous probe scheduling.

## Long-horizon phase
After P bootstrap, ordinary episodes use **zero supplied rehearsal rows**. V1 must nominate one-step rehearsal from its current learned relations, nominate an intent, reauthorize EFFECT, execute through the existing environment adapter, and record the actual outcome.

At the causal shift:
1. old P relations initially continue to predict the old consequence;
2. actual N outcomes contradict them;
3. predictive-currentness pressure must produce a drift witness;
4. old relations become stale;
5. zero-row rehearsal must fail closed while no replacement relation is current;
6. replacement candidates must arise from lived N outcomes;
7. external qualification may qualify the replacements;
8. zero-row operation resumes on the new current relations.

## Pre-registered interpretation

- zero-row action reuse after bootstrap;
- stale relation refusal after the causal shift;
- localized relearning/requalification without global reset;
- restart recovery without automatic authority gain;
- referent ambiguity localized rather than guessed;

## Research repair — learned-relation currentness propagates into durable rehearsal reuse
The existing `counterfactual_rehearsal_status` owner is extended narrowly. For each transition digest already carried by a durable proposal, it checks whether that digest corresponds to one or more qualified learned predictive relations. If so, at least one matching learned relation must remain current. If every matching learned relation is stale, proposal status becomes `UNKNOWN_INCOMPLETE` with `REHEARSAL_LEARNED_RELATION_NOT_CURRENT:<relation-id>`.

No new registry, manager, ID, semantic ontology, or policy is introduced. The repair composes existing proposal transition ancestry with the already-owned `_action_outcome_relation_current` predicate.

Supplied-row proposals remain on their historical premise/currentness checks when no learned-registry digest matches. Projection-scoped routing remains separately governed by its qualified binding/currentness path.

Expected repair law:
`DURABLE_REHEARSAL_REUSE_DOES_NOT_OUTLIVE_THE_CURRENTNESS_OF_ITS_OWNED_LEARNED_TRANSITION_PREMISE`.
