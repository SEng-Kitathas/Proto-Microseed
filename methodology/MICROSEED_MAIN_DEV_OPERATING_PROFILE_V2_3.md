# Microseed Main-Dev Operating Profile v2.3

Inherits v2.2 and integrates only the narrow MS1353–1377 TRCH result.

## Earned commitment interface

Decision/inference-bearing state may be projected onto a `RelationalCommitment` whose coarse commitment is `YES | NO | UNKNOWN`, interpreted as query-relative **premise licensing**, not world truth. Referential binding and predicate applicability are separate orthogonal relations and each preserves honest `YES | NO | UNKNOWN` rather than a single `BOUND | NULL` gate.

The richer substrate remains legal and expected: continuous/probabilistic evidence, provenance, time, causal/evidential ancestry, lifecycle, currentness, authority and typed domain relations are not collapsed into the three commitment symbols. `UNKNOWN(conflict)` and `UNKNOWN(ignorance)` may share the same commitment only when their distinct evidence topology remains recoverable and continues to change downstream behavior appropriately.

## Integration ceiling

This is a **common primitive + adapter layer**, not a bulk rewrite. Existing `FeasibilityState`, `EpistemicStatus`, `QualificationState`, `EpistemicDeficitState`, bearing kinds, authority and currentness machinery remain authoritative native state until differential tests prove individual replacements lossless.

No universal Strong-Kleene composition law, fourth commitment value, Bayesian rejection, binary-absence ontology, semantic proposition system, symbolic reasoner, or truth authority is promoted. Authority/recency do not settle truth; cycles are not invalid by default; loss of one support does not imply NULL; recursive reification does not itself constitute metacognition.

## Portfolio return

The architectural interrupt is satisfied narrowly enough to prevent further incompatible local commitment conventions. Global attention returns to the deferred whole-system `deliberation → action → outcome` closure frontier, now constrained to preserve the TRCH commitment interface and its abstention/currentness/authority separations. Local adapter expansion remains technical debt and may only proceed by differential losslessness evidence.
