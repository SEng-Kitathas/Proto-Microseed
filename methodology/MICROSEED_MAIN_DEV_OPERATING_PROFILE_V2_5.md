# Microseed Main-Dev Operating Profile v2.5

Inherits v2.4 and integrates only the narrow MS1403–1427 action/outcome predictive-learning result.

## Experience-derived predictive learning

The learning record is anchored on **what was actually executed and externally observed**. The intended or predicted outcome is provenance/prediction-error context only and is never the consequence-learning label. A failed intention may therefore be a successful learning event.

Repeated executed-action/actual-outcome evidence may nominate an opaque predictive-transition candidate under supplied support/consistency bounds. One outcome is evidence, not law; passive co-occurrence is not an action consequence; unresolved hidden mixtures abstain rather than averaging into a flattering relation.

Candidates are `MODEL_OUTPUT_ONLY` and carry no truth, causal-theorem, qualification, execution, or semantic-goal authority. Admission requires independent external content-bound holdout evidence with no proposal-evidence overlap. A qualified relation remains `EVIDENCE_BOUND_PREDICTIVE_RELATION_ONLY`, is rechecked against capability/frame/episode/value/topology/coordination currentness, and may then feed the existing bounded rehearsal substrate.

## Authority and history

Prediction error creates **model pressure, not model authority**. Drift can make a learned relation stale for current use without deleting the historical relation or rewriting its original evidence. No automatic model switch/rewrite, general causal learner, semantic goal learner, or self-qualification path is integrated.

## Parent→Child donor scars retained

`INTENDED_EFFECT != OBSERVED_EFFECT`; `FAILED_INTENTION != FAILED_LEARNING_EVENT`; `REQUEST != EXECUTION_AUTHORITY`; `PARENT_MODEL != CURRENT_CHILD_CAPABILITY`; `IMAGINED_STATE != ACTUAL_STATE`; `ONE_OUTCOME != QUALIFIED_PREDICTIVE_LAW`. Grand/Parent→Child material remains donor pressure only.

## Next whole-system pressure

Use a learned/qualified law successfully, expose it to unannounced real drift, stale current use without erasing history, nominate/requalify a replacement only from actual outcomes, and resume bounded behavior. Prediction error alone must not switch the model.
