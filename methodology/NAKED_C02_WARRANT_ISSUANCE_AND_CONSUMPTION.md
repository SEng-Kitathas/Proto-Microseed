Warrant authority must be irreversibly consumed/reserved **before** physical EFFECT. If consumed after effect, a crash between effect and bookkeeping can replay the same experimental authority. Pre-effect consumption may lose an experiment if a crash occurs before effect, but it fails safe and does not double-authorize.
