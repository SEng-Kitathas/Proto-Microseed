# Microseed Main-Dev operating profile v0.2

## No new religion

The core method is unchanged:

```text
HSP
 ↓
Research / Loop+
 ↓
problem-space expansion
 ↓
OARR culling
 ↓
PDVER
 ↙            ↘
Research Arm   Embodiment Arm
 ↘            ↙
 shared evidence / scars
          ↓
    Semantic Helix
          ↓
 Attention Reservoir
          ↓
       recurse
```

OARR is still not Loop+, the Helix, PDVER, or HSP. Research and Embodiment remain sibling evidence sources. The Helix still earns the *local* next question; the Reservoir still chooses what the program actually attacks next.

## What MS828-852 improved

The campaign exposed a process failure mode rather than a need for a replacement method: a long sequence of locally excellent faculty experiments can accumulate while integration defects and capability-closure questions remain underexercised.

So v0.2 makes several previously implicit disciplines explicit:

1. **Local frontier ≠ portfolio selection.** A Helix-earned successor is live, not automatically scheduled. The Reservoir compares it with lateral faculty pressure, embodiment debt, reopen triggers, and whole-system needs.
2. **Research survivor ≠ entity promotion.** Research and Embodiment hard-stop. Main-Dev then adjudicates whether evidence warrants no change, research-only retention, entity evolution, method refinement, deferral, or rejection.
3. **Integration debt is first-class evidence.** A code/model inconsistency such as MS841-843 is not buried as a note under the next research question.
4. **Embodiment reports capability-closure delta.** It must state what became newly reachable, whether a qualified whole became a part again, and whether second-order composition survived. “All components still pass” is insufficient.
5. **Whole-enough targeted ablation becomes periodic, not endgame-only.** This operationalizes the already-existing Research↔Embodiment doctrine without turning the umbrella into an organ chart.
6. **Cross-family collision pressure.** A productive faculty family may continue, but repeated same-family extensions must periodically justify themselves against a lateral collision likely to change the organism's reachable capability space.
7. **Prelingual anti-cheat remains mandatory.** Convenient labels/cues must be inverted, removed or permuted when they could be doing the cognition for the system.
8. **Evolution must replay the scar that justified it.** Main-Dev source changes are not complete until the originating hostile specimen and existing regression suite both survive.

## Main-Dev authority

Main-Dev is not a third evidence arm. It is the integration authority that acts *after* the evidence-producing arms stop.

```text
Research/Embodiment evidence
        ↓ hard stop
Main-Dev integration adjudication
        ↓ if warranted
entity/method evolution
        ↓
originating-scar replay + regressions
        ↓
new evidence returns to HSP
```

This keeps the historical firewall intact: a research mechanism does not silently promote itself merely because it produced the code that found the result.

## Why this is still prelingual

The active target remains a compositional developmental capability ecology. Developer-facing names are instrumentation. They are not evidence that Microseed uses words as the substrate of referents, affordances, episodes, goals, capabilities or reasoning.

Language stays behind the admission gate until the prelingual substrate demonstrates a real limit for which linguistic structure buys capability without simply injecting human semantic answers.
