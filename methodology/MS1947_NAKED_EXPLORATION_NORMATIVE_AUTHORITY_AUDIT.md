## Candidate A — emergency regulatory necessity
Candidate law:
> If current constitutional regulatory pressure is nonzero, no current known action predicts improvement, and a represented current EFFECT action is feasible but unmodeled, permit one sample of that action.

### Apparent attraction
This would connect an already constitutional regulatory deficit to developmental experimentation without a generic curiosity scalar.

### New normative content exposed
The existing value contract licenses **pressure measurement**, not the rule:
`PRESSURE + NO_KNOWN_SOLUTION -> TRY_UNKNOWN_ACTION`.

That implication does not exist in current authority owners.

### Hostile counterexample
An unmodeled action may have a catastrophic or strongly adverse downstream consequence. Lack of a known improving route does not make the unknown route preferable to abstention.

Therefore:
- `REGULATORY_PRESSURE != EXPERIMENT_PERMISSION`;
- `NO_KNOWN_IMPROVING_ROUTE != TRY_ANY_UNKNOWN_ROUTE`.

Disposition: **NOT DERIVABLE / NEW NORMATIVE POLICY REQUIRED**.

## Candidate B — bounded-risk unknown-action experiment
Candidate law:
> Permit one sample if the represented action is current/qualified and its risk is independently known to remain inside a bounded safety envelope.

### Apparent attraction
This avoids treating ignorance itself as permission and resembles safe-exploration/runtime-assurance structure.

### Current gap
Current capability `hazards` and boundary fields are content declarations, not an independently qualified quantitative/current downstream safety envelope. A qualified EFFECT capability establishes the primitive effect boundary, not all downstream world consequences.

Making this route lawful therefore requires at least one additional authority-bearing premise:
- independently qualified safe-set/risk-envelope/shield evidence; or
- external intervention authorization that incorporates such evidence.

That is a legitimate architecture branch, but it is not derived from current NAKED state.

Disposition:
- **NAKED CURRENT ARCHITECTURE: NOT DERIVABLE**;
- **EQUIPPED/FEDERATED: COMPATIBLE WITH MS1926 DESIGN**, still assisted/build-gated.

Preserve:
`BOUNDED_EFFECT_CAPABILITY != BOUNDED_DOWNSTREAM_RISK`.

## Candidate C — finite developmental experiment budget
Candidate law:
> Give the organism a finite quota/budget of exploratory actions that may be spent on represented unknown effects.

### Apparent attraction
Finite quota limits irreversible exploration and avoids a global unbounded curiosity loop.

### New normative content exposed
A budget does not explain why spending a unit is permitted; it creates a new constitutional objective/policy saying exploratory information is worth physical intervention up to some amount.

Even a one-use budget is still:
`EXPLICIT_EXPLORATION_PERMISSION`.

If externally supplied, it is essentially the MS1926 equipped warrant class. If self-owned, it is a new NAKED constitutional authority.

Disposition: **NOT DERIVABLE / NEW CONSTITUTIONAL COMMITMENT**.
