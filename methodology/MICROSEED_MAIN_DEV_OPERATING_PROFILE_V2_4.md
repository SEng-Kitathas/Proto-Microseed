# Microseed Main-Dev Operating Profile v2.4

Inherits v2.3 and integrates only the narrow MS1378–1402 whole-system control-loop result.

## Bounded control-loop closure

A current rehearsal proposal may yield a TRCH `YES` commitment for **only its next action** when the proposal is current, applies to the externally observed opaque control state, and predicts lower current constitutional regulatory pressure. The commitment is premise licensing only: it grants **no execution, truth, qualification, scheduling, policy, or semantic-intention authority**.

Actual execution requires a separately current externally-qualified capability whose authority is `EFFECT`, plus an exact current `QueryObligation` requiring `EFFECT` in the same operational scope. The capability handler result is not observation. A content-bound external `OBSERVATION_ONLY` outcome is required before state/value/evidence may update.

The loop is receding-horizon: **one action → actual observation → re-deliberate from reality**. A multi-step rehearsal is never executed open-loop. If the observed first-step outcome violates the prediction, that becomes a NO commitment about the model prediction and forces re-deliberation; it does not rewrite reality, invent a replacement model, or self-disqualify/qualify a relation.

## TRCH constraints preserved

Binding, applicability and commitment remain orthogonal. UNKNOWN is not coerced to NO; binding/applicability uncertainty produces different developmental pressure from explicit rejection or inapplicability. YES remains commitment, not world truth.

## Non-promotions

No general policy learner, general planner, continuous controller, semantic goal/intention ontology, endogenous action vocabulary, endogenous opaque state construction, automatic multi-step execution, or action-outcome self-qualification is integrated. Restart preserves action-loop history but does not restore executable capability handlers/contracts.

## Next whole-system pressure

Actual action outcomes now become evidence and current state/value, but they do not yet lawfully update/requalify the predictive transition substrate that future rehearsal consumes. Global attention therefore moves to experience→predictive-relation update closure, preserving external qualification/currentness and explicit UNKNOWN under insufficient evidence.
