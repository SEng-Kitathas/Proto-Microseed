## Additive refinements from MS878–902

1. **Frame currentness is developmental ancestry.** If a learned/qualified capability derives its evidence from an operational action/effect frame, the frame and its epoch are prerequisites of that capability claim. Candidate bytes remaining identical is insufficient.
2. **Operational-frame currentness is relation currentness, not surface identity.** Pure sensor-channel remapping does not automatically imply developmental frame change when the action/effect relation is operationally equivalent. Conversely, stable channel names cannot keep a materially changed frame current.
3. **Pending admission rechecks frame dependencies.** External qualification does not freeze the world. Admission must reject a proposal if a frame epoch changed after nomination/qualification.
4. **Admitted frame-bound closure stales transitively.** A material frame change invalidates derived capabilities and later capabilities that depend on them, while historical evidence remains historical fact.
5. **Bounded frame recovery ≠ general sensorimotor-frame construction.** Strong results in small linear/relational regimes cannot be promoted into a general frame learner, particularly when hostile dimensionality/noise drives abstention.
6. **Identifiability ≠ tractable search.** A unique lawful correspondence may exist while the search required to find it becomes developmentally/economically unacceptable. Search economics remain part of the evidence state.
7. **Higher-level episode/trace grouping remains an explicit assistance denominator.** Removing lower-level action/effect-frame assistance does not license silently treating pre-grouped experiential traces as self-constructed cognition.
8. **Cross-family reactivation remains evidence-driven.** Temporal/episode abstraction becomes selected now because capability discovery demonstrably depends on supplied episode grouping, not because the umbrella is being executed as a waterfall.

## Main-Dev integration rule

Research/Embodiment hard-stop before source mutation. Main-Dev consumes only the narrow architecture warranted by the campaign, replays the scar that forced the change, and runs the inherited regression/ancestry suite before the evolved entity becomes current.

MS878–902 warrants first-class frame lifecycle/currentness dependencies. It does **not** warrant embedding the campaign's bounded frame learner as Microseed truth machinery, general sensorimotor-frame construction, language, or persistent selfhood.
