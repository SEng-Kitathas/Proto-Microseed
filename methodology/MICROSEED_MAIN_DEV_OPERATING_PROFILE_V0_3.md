## Additive refinements from MS853–877

1. **Proposal generator ≠ truth engine.** A bounded endogenous discovery mechanism may optimize useful search/recall and still overpropose. False proposal load is an engineering/evidence metric, not permission to let the generator grade itself.
2. **Proposal evidence ≠ qualification evidence.** Evidence explaining why Microseed nominated a candidate is developmental ancestry. Independent Research/HSP evidence may arrive later and qualify that same immutable proposal without rewriting its original evidence state.
3. **Evidence resolved ≠ evidence supportive.** Content identity and ledger resolution are necessary, not sufficient. Qualification requires explicitly supportive epistemic dispositions; `UNKNOWN_INCOMPLETE`, `UNSUPPORTED`, `VIOLATED`, etc. cannot false-green merely because their bytes resolve.
4. **Pending candidate currentness is first-class.** Candidate bytes can remain identical while a prerequisite capability changes. Admission therefore rechecks prerequisite qualification and epochs after external qualification and before registration.
5. **Deferred frontiers may reactivate by dependency evidence.** A previously deferred local frontier becomes globally higher-priority when a later cross-family survivor empirically depends on it. This is not the Helix overriding the Reservoir; it is new evidence changing the Reservoir state.
6. **Discovery reports proposal economics.** Endogenous-discovery campaigns report proposal count/load, ranking quality, external acceptance precision/recall where measurable, and search-language assistance.
7. **Prelingual anti-cheat remains active.** The new discovery bridge uses opaque capability handles and numerical effects, but its trace boundaries, effect coordinates, motif language and thresholds are still supplied. Those denominators remain research targets rather than being relabeled as “learned cognition.”

## Main-Dev integration rule

Research/Embodiment still hard-stop before source mutation. Main-Dev may then integrate only what the evidence warrants, replay the originating scars, and return the evolved entity to HSP.

MS853–877 warrants a bounded proposal generator and admission-firewall repairs. It does **not** warrant self-qualification, general capability discovery, self-constructed action/effect frames, language, or persistent selfhood.
